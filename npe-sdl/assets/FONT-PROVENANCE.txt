The production program reads these extracted tables directly. It does not
load the source PRG or NAND_IMAGE at runtime. The historical Noto-derived
gbk12.bin is not used or included in this package; its OFL license does not
apply to these native tables.

The executable also contains 33 original 12-row ASCII control/DEL glyphs
which are absent from the NPEFONT1 layout. They are copied unchanged from
the same verified PRG at 0x30F000 + code * 32 (first 24 bytes per glyph).
Their generated source is native12_control_glyphs.inc; reproduce it with
extract_np560_gbk_font.py --controls. The two font assets above are unchanged.
